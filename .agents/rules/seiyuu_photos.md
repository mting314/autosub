# Rule: Never Generate Images of Seiyuu (Voice Actresses)

1. **NEVER use AI image generation (`generate_image`) to create photos, avatars, or depictions of real seiyuu (voice actresses)**. Generating synthetic images of real voice actresses is strictly prohibited and disrespectful.
2. **Always fetch official cast photos** from official project websites (e.g. `https://www.lovelive-anime.jp/yuigaoka/cast/`), official promotional materials, or official media releases.
3. **If official photos are unavailable**, use sleek placeholder graphics with the character's official color, initials, and name.
